## Script changes

- `import-suno.py` starts a stanza at a section label (`[Verse`, `[Chorus`,
  `[Bridge`, ...) that follows a sung line: The Story of Me's sheet has no
  blank lines between sections, and would have come in as two giant stanzas.
  No existing sheet has such a label mid-stanza, so re-imports are unchanged.
- `import-suno.py` applies the song's pairs from `corrections.json`. Look
  Again's voice cues are written in parentheses ("(Agent - male, vocoder)"),
  which the app would sing; one `lookagain` pair turns a parenthesised line
  starting Human, Agent, Together or Traditional into a `[label]`. Sung
  parentheses elsewhere ("(again, again)") are untouched.
- `suno-catalog.py` strips the per-song exports' " - usesuno.com" from zip
  members, so a short title such as Lift Me Higher finds its download.
- The older zips the catalogue lists are no longer on disk (their worktree
  is gone), so after the rerun the previous `files.downloads` lists were
  merged back in; only Telemetry gained an entry.
