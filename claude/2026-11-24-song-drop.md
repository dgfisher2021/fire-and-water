## Inferred, worth a look

- **The subtitle** "(Anti-Gospel Version)" is from the style prompt
  ("cinematic dark pop, anti gospel ... massive gospel choir"). The file is
  `i-refuse-to-give-up-anti-gospel.mp3`; renaming the file later needs a
  new `pnpm media:sync`, renaming the title does not.
- **Heavy is the Head (Rise to Sovereignty)** keeps Suno's lowercase "is";
  the original in the app is "Heavy Is the Head". It sits in You Made Me
  More with `heavy` as briefed, though half of it is Signal to Sovereignty,
  which lives in More Than Enough. Its dedication is "By Dustin", not the
  original's "For Love Love, from Dustin": the verses are the villain
  ballad's, addressed to someone else.
- **Chosen on Purpose** is in Hope and Rising beside I Choose Me. More Than
  Enough would also hold it ("the names they gave").
- **Voices** come from the style prompts: Heavy "sultry soulful female lead
  ... stacked gospel-inflected harmonies"; Refuse "close male vocals,
  swelling choir responses"; Writing "close, breathy melodic female lead in
  the user's recorded voice"; Chosen "intimate close-mic whispers" and
  "vocoder-treated choirs". None was listened to.
- **Covers:** Writing the Story of Me has the same cover as The Story of Me
  (941x1672, fire and water), so its voice takes the fire side (hue 36)
  where the older song holds the waves (245). The Refuse cover on Suno is
  only 306x424 (an ink drawing of a hand), scaled up to 739x1024; it is
  soft at full size. Its voice is a pale paper tone (hue 70, chroma 0.05)
  since the drawing is grey. Chosen on Purpose moved from the picker's 259
  to 276 with more chroma, off Drifting into Space (252); Heavy to a
  fuller gold (90, 0.13), off the original Refuse (82).
- The album blurbs were left as they are.

## Sheets

- **Chosen on Purpose** was exported with each section on one line
  ("[Verse 1] I came up like a bruise Still learning my name ..."). The
  importer would have made each a single line, so the sheet was re-broken
  by hand at the capitals that start each line (every break falls before a
  capitalised word; the words were checked one for one against the export)
  and imported from a helper zip. Importing the original zip again reports
  "sheet differs, kept".
- **I Refuse to Give Up:** the importer matches a sheet to a song by audio
  stem, and the export's stem is the original's, so the same text was
  imported under the version's stem from the helper zip. `refusegospel.json`
  is identical to `refuse.json`.
- **Heavy is the Head (Rise to Sovereignty)** carries `[singer A]`,
  `[singer B]` and `[transition]` labels, as Signal to Sovereignty does. Its
  last three lines ("Bow to your reflection ... straighten out your crown")
  do not seem to be in the recording: Whisper heard nothing after 345 s and
  the forced alignment scores them 0.03. They are kept on the sheet.
- **Writing the Story of Me** is as written, lowercase line starts, "hard to
  bare" and a double space included.
- The bridge of I Refuse to Give Up has one strong word ("Fuck that
  voice"), as the original's sheet does.
