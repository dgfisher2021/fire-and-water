## Left as is

- **Dust UI kit type scale.** The kit sets its mobile text in rem at 0.46
  to 0.72rem: nav labels 8px, page-header eyebrow 8px and subtitle 9.3px,
  list group labels 7.4px, list row labels 9.6px, footers 8px, media player
  times 9px. That is small against platform norms (iOS caption 11pt, body
  17pt). The kit's own scale knob is the density preset on the root font
  size (compact 14px, comfortable 16px, spacious 17px), which also scales
  Tailwind's rem spacing, so the app cannot enlarge kit text alone without
  forking. The right fix is upstream in dust-ui (a larger mobile type scale
  or a fourth density), decided by Dustin.
- **Kit icon buttons at 36px and the media player's skip buttons at 32px.**
  Under the 44px guideline but the kit's sizes; the play toggle and rows
  are 44px or more.
- **Carousel dots at 8px.** The whole row is the target; the dots opt out
  of the touch rule on purpose.

## Verified

Both schemes, phone and frame: no console errors, no horizontal overflow,
the Compare tab reopens the swapped pair after a detour through More and
follows the music once another song plays, the action sheet's Compare
entry opens the partner pair, long titles clamp, list titles truncate.
