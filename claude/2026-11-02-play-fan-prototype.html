The fan: which one feels right?

Three takes on the same move, all live. Same rules in each so you can compare the look.

Tap the button: play or pause, as today. Hold it or swipe up: the fan opens; slide onto a control and let go to use it. Let go on nothing and the fan stays open for taps; tap outside to close. On the dial, drag along the rim to scrub. "Show me" just plays the opening.
